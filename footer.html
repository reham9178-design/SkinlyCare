Explore answers to the most frequently asked questions about skincare and product usage.

Is sunscreen necessary every day?

Yes, daily sunscreen protects your skin from UV damage and premature ageing.

How often should I moisturise my skin?

Moisturising your skin twice a day can help keep it hydrated, soft and healthy.

Can I use Vitamin C every day?

Yes, Vitamin C can usually be used daily as part of your skincare routine.

How long does it take to see skincare results?

Most skincare products need several weeks of consistent use before noticeable results appear.

Should I wash my face twice a day?

Washing your face morning and evening is generally enough for most skin types.

Can I use skincare products on sensitive skin?

Yes, but choose gentle products and always introduce new products gradually.
